# Your first three smart-home purchases

**Subject:** Your first three smart-home purchases

Most smart-home frustration starts with buying too much, too early.

Start with three things: a Home Assistant hub, a Zigbee/Thread radio, and a small set of contact or motion sensors. Then build one automation you will actually notice—like a hallway light that turns on dimly after midnight.

That tiny win teaches you more than a cart full of cloud-dependent gadgets. It also keeps the system local, replaceable, and under your control.

**This week's rule:** don't buy the fourth device until the first three work together.
