# I built a physical inbox from a receipt printer

**Subject:** I built a physical inbox from a receipt printer

An ESP8266, a thermal receipt printer, and a tiny local web page became a physical household inbox: type a note on your phone, tap send, and a timestamped slip prints instantly.

The fun engineering detail was orientation. Because the paper feeds out the top, I enabled 180-degree printing and reversed the wrapped lines so the finished note reads naturally in your hand.

Small projects like this are why I keep inexpensive development boards around. They turn a vague “wouldn't it be useful if…” into something real over a weekend.
