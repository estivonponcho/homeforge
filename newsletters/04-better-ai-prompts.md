# The four-part prompt that fixes most AI output

**Subject:** The four-part prompt that fixes most AI output

Most disappointing AI output is not a model problem. It is a briefing problem.

Use four parts:

- **Role:** who the model should act as.
- **Goal:** the concrete outcome, not merely the topic.
- **Context:** the facts, examples, and constraints it needs.
- **Format:** the exact shape of the answer you can use.

For example: “Act as a meticulous technical editor. Rewrite this setup guide for a first-time Home Assistant user. Preserve every command, flag anything unsafe, and return clean Markdown with a short troubleshooting section.”

The format instruction is the part people skip most—and the part that often saves the most editing.
