# Affiliate Disclosure

**Last updated: 2026-09-13**

Some links in this project may become **affiliate links**. If you click one and
buy something, this project may earn a commission — at **no extra cost to you**.
The price you pay is exactly the same.

This is how the project pays for itself. It does not change what gets
recommended: picks are chosen because they're worth owning, not because of the
commission. Plenty of items here (open-source software, direct-only hardware)
pay nothing at all and are recommended anyway.

## Programs this project may participate in

- **Amazon Associates** (planned)
- **Bambu Lab Affiliate Program**
- **Prusa Research Affiliate Program**
- Occasional manufacturer or retailer programs (Shelly, Emporia, etc.)

## Your trust comes first

- Affiliate links are never hidden. Where a link pays, it's disclosed.
- A recommendation is never sold. No "sponsored placement" spots.
- If something stops being worth recommending, it comes off the list —
  commission or not.
