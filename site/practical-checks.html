Before you buy / One useful test

Make the unknowns visible.

Two free printable worksheets. No email signup required. Use them to record what is known, what is missing and what needs testing.

Compare the complete cost

Record the exact configurations, required components, shipping and taxes. Mark an unknown cost as unknown, not zero. Equal cost does not establish equal capability.

Download the complete-cost worksheet (PDF)

The fictional illustration shows $249 + $40 RAM + $50 storage = $339 versus a $329 bundle with both included. Taxes, shipping and other requirements are excluded from that illustration; these are not live prices.
