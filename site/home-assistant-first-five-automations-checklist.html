Your first five useful Home Assistant automations

Build these in order. Test each one for several days before adding the next.

Read the full beginner guide

□1. Leak alert

Hardware: one leak sensor near a washer, sink, water heater, or sump.

Trigger: moisture detected. Action: urgent phone notification naming the location.

Test: touch the sensor contacts with a damp paper towel and confirm the alert arrives.

□2. Night path lighting

Hardware: motion sensor and dimmable light.

Trigger: motion after bedtime. Action: light on at 5–15%, then off after vacancy.

Fallback: the normal wall switch must continue to work.

□3. Door-aware lighting

Hardware: contact sensor and nearby light.

Trigger: door opens after sunset. Action: light on for a short, useful interval.

Test: confirm daytime openings do nothing.

□4. Appliance finished

Hardware: energy-monitoring smart plug rated for the appliance.

Trigger: power stays below the chosen threshold after a running period. Action: send a finished notification.

Safety: never use an underrated plug or automate a heating appliance unattended.

□5. Bedtime scene

Hardware: button, NFC tag, or dashboard control.

Action: turn off common-area lights, adjust selected devices, and confirm completion.

Test: exclude anything that should never be shut off automatically.

Before expanding

□ Backups are scheduled and copied off the Home Assistant device.
□ Devices have clear room-based names.
□ Low-battery and unavailable-device alerts exist.
□ Every automation has an easy manual override.
□ Public screenshots hide addresses, cameras, tokens, and precise presence history.
