How we cover this

  • Documented: an original statement or identified reporting supports the event.
  • Attributed: a researcher or company makes a claim. We name the source; attribution is not independent proof.
  • Unresolved: implementation, causes, predictions or outcomes have not been established.

We distinguish an evaluation result from a real-world incident, a resignation from proof of misconduct, and a safety promise from a verified safeguard. Company statements and advocacy publications are identified as such. New evidence can change a conclusion.
