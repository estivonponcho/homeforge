The HomeForge list

A hand-picked kit for the overlapping worlds of the smart home, the homelab, the 3D-printing bench, and running your own AI. No scraped catalogs, no filler — every item here is something worth owning, with an honest one-line take on why.

Some links are affiliate links (e.g. Amazon Associates) — if you buy through them we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only list gear worth owning.

🏠 Smart Home

Local-first home automation. Bias toward Home Assistant, Zigbee/Thread/Matter, and gear you own rather than rent from a cloud.

Hubs & Coordinators
  • Home Assistant Green ⭐$
    The no-fuss way to run Home Assistant — plug in, power on, done. The default recommendation for anyone starting out.
  • Home Assistant Yellow$$
    PoE, an M.2 slot, and a built-in Zigbee/Thread radio. The upgrade pick when you want one box on the network.
  • Raspberry Pi 5$
    Still the most flexible base for a DIY HA server. Pair with an NVMe HAT and it flies.
Radios & Coordinators
  • Home Assistant Connect ZBT-1 ⭐$
    The SkyConnect successor — a USB Zigbee + Thread stick that keeps your radio off the main board and easy to relocate.
  • Sonoff Zigbee 3.0 USB Dongle Plus (ZBDongle-E)$
    The budget coordinator everyone recommends. Flash it, stick it on a USB extension, forget about it.
Sensors & Presence
  • Aqara Door & Window Sensor$
    Cheap, tiny, reliable Zigbee contact sensors. The gateway drug of home automation.
  • Aqara Presence Sensor FP2 ⭐$$
    mMWave presence done well — knows you're in the room even when you're sitting still. Zone mapping is the killer feature.
  • Everything Presence Lite$
    Open, ESPHome-based mmWave presence board. The tinkerer's answer to closed-box presence sensors.
  • SONOFF Zigbee Temp & Humidity Sensor$
    Bench-tested: cheap, accurate Zigbee climate sensors with a display. Great for rooms, fridges, and the 3D-printing enclosure.
Switches, Plugs & Power
  • Shelly Plus 1PM ⭐$
    Relay + power metering that hides behind an existing switch. Local API, ESPHome-flashable, MQTT — the enthusiast favorite.
  • ThirdReality Zigbee Smart Plug$
    Compact Zigbee plugs with power monitoring at a price that lets you buy a ten-pack.
  • Emporia Vue Energy Monitor$$
    Whole-panel, per-circuit energy monitoring for a fraction of the utility's price. Feeds beautifully into HA dashboards.
Lighting
  • Philips Hue$$
    Still the gold standard for reliable, fast smart lighting. Expensive, but it just works — and now speaks Matter.
  • Govee smart lights$
    Bright, affordable smart lighting with a huge range of strips, bulbs, and room kits. A strong value pick when local control is not the only priority.
  • Tuya smart lights$
    A broad ecosystem of affordable bulbs, switches, and light strips. Check the exact device integration before buying because Tuya hardware varies by model.
  • WLEDFree / DIY
    Free firmware that turns an ESP32 + LED strip into gorgeous, HA-controllable lighting. The DIY entry point.
Voice & DIY
  • Home Assistant Voice Preview Edition$
    Local voice control with no cloud eavesdropping. The privacy-first alternative to Alexa/Google.
  • ESPHome + ESP32Free / DIY
    The framework that turns cheap microcontrollers into custom HA sensors and controllers. Where the real fun starts.
Bench & DIY Electronics
  • ESP32 (ESP-WROOM-32) Dev Boards ⭐$
    Bench-tested. Dual-core Wi-Fi + Bluetooth for a couple bucks. Buy a 2-pack — you'll always want another.
  • WeMos D1 Mini (ESP8266)$
    Bench-tested. The tiny board behind my Wi-Fi thermal-printer build. Perfect for single-purpose gadgets.
  • ELEGOO Breadboard Kit$
    Bench-tested. Solderless boards in a few sizes. Prototype before you commit anything to solder.
  • Dupont Jumper Wire Kit$
    Bench-tested. M/M, M/F, F/F ribbon cables. You will use every one of these and still want more.
  • 0.96" OLED Display (SSD1306, I2C)$
    Bench-tested. Crisp little status screens for any ESP project — two data wires and you're showing readouts.
  • Logic Level Converter (3.3V ↔ 5V)$
    Bench-tested. The part beginners skip and then fry a board. Bridges 3.3V ESP pins to 5V peripherals safely.
  • Silicone Soldering Mat$
    Bench-tested. Heat-resistant surface with magnetic wells for screws. Saves your desk and your sanity.
  • 37-in-1 Sensor Kit$
    Bench-tested. A grab-bag of sensors and modules to learn with. The fastest way to find your next project.

🖥️ Homelab & Self-Hosting

The boxes, drives, network, and software to run your own services and stop paying rent to someone else's subscription.

Mini PCs & SBCs
  • Beelink Mini PC ⭐$$
    Tiny, quiet, cheap x86 boxes that run Proxmox or Docker beautifully. The homelab starter that isn't a Pi.
  • Minisforum Mini PC$$
    Step-up mini PCs with real cores and RAM headroom — for when one node stops being enough.
  • Inovato Quadra$
    A shockingly cheap ARM box that sips power — perfect for a always-on Tailscale/AdGuard/utility node.
NAS & Storage
  • Synology DiskStation$$$
    The most polished turnkey NAS. Pay the premium if you want storage that your family can use too.
  • UGREEN NASync ⭐$$
    The value NAS challenger — strong hardware, and you can run TrueNAS/Unraid on it if you outgrow the stock OS.
  • WD Red Plus NAS Drives$$
    CMR NAS drives built for 24/7 spinning. Buy from mixed batches, always keep a cold spare.
  • Samsung T7 Portable SSD$$
    Fast, pocketable USB-C SSD — great for Time Machine, VM backups, or a bootable rescue drive.
Networking
  • UniFi (Ubiquiti)$$$
    Prosumer networking with a gorgeous controller. Once you VLAN off your IoT gear you won't go back.
  • GL.iNet Travel Router ⭐$
    Pocket router running OpenWrt with WireGuard/Tailscale baked in. The remote-access swiss army knife.
  • TailscaleFree tier
    Zero-config mesh VPN that makes your whole lab reachable from anywhere without opening a single port.
Self-Hosted Software
  • Proxmox VEFree
    Free, rock-solid hypervisor. The foundation most serious homelabs are built on.
  • Immich ⭐Free
    Self-hosted Google Photos replacement that's genuinely good now. The reason a lot of people build a homelab.
  • JellyfinFree
    Fully free, no-account media server. Owns your movies and shows without a subscription.
  • AdGuard HomeFree
    Network-wide ad and tracker blocking. Point your DHCP at it and every device gets cleaner.
  • Frigate NVRFree
    Local, AI-powered camera recording with real object detection — no cloud subscription, no monthly fee.
Power Protection
  • CyberPower UPS$$
    Keeps your NAS and HA box alive through blips and lets them shut down cleanly. Non-negotiable once data matters.
Bench & Maintenance
  • Phomemo D30 Label Maker$
    Bench-tested. Label your cables, drives, and bins. The cheapest upgrade to a homelab you'll actually maintain.
  • ARCTIC MX-4 Thermal Paste$
    Bench-tested. The reliable, non-conductive default for repasting a mini PC, NAS, or GPU that's running hot.

🧵 3D Printing

Printers, filament, upgrades and tools that earn their place on the bench — tuned toward the Bambu ecosystem but not married to it.

Printers
  • Bambu Lab A1 mini ⭐$
    The best first printer, full stop. Auto-calibration means you print instead of tinker — unless you want to.
  • Bambu Lab A1$$
    Full-size bed, same hands-off ease as the mini, optional AMS lite for multicolor. The value sweet spot.
  • Bambu Lab P1S ⭐$$
    Enclosed CoreXY speed demon for ABS/ASA and engineering filaments. The upgrade most people land on.
  • Prusa Core One$$$
    The open, repairable, made-to-last alternative. Buy it if you value the ecosystem and want to own your machine.
Filament
  • Bambu PLA Basic$
    Consistent, RFID-tagged filament that the AMS reads automatically. The path of least resistance.
  • Polymaker PolyTerra PLA$
    Matte finish, great color range, eco-spool. A favorite for parts you want to look intentional.
  • Overture PETG ⭐$
    Reliable, affordable PETG for functional parts that need to survive heat and stress. The go-to workhorse.
  • ELEGOO Rapid PETG ⭐$
    Bench-tested (I go through this by the case): high-speed PETG that prints clean and cheap. My default workhorse spool.
  • DEEPLEE PLA (2kg / 4kg)$
    Bench-tested. Big-spool value PLA that winds neatly and doesn't clog. What I reach for when cost-per-gram matters.
Upgrades & Accessories
  • Bambu AMS$$
    Multi-color and multi-material without babysitting. Also just a great auto-feeding filament pantry.
  • Hardened Steel Nozzle$
    The five-dollar upgrade that saves your nozzle the first time you print carbon-fiber or glow filament.
Filament Drying & Storage
  • SUNLU S4 Filament Dryer ⭐$$
    Bench-tested — the one I use. 4-spool capacity, 3 circulation fans, 350W PTC heater to 70°C, with humidity readout. Wet filament ruins prints; this fixed my stringing and brittle parts.
  • Fonday Rechargeable Silica Gel Desiccant Beads$
    Bench-tested. Color-indicating beads (orange → green when spent) that I keep in the AMS and every storage bin. Bake them dry and reuse for years.
  • Shazo Airtight Storage Containers$
    Bench-tested — the airtight containers I actually store spools in. Add a scoop of desiccant and filament stays dry for months. Cheapest insurance against wasted filament.
Tools & Software
  • OrcaSlicerFree
    The community slicer with the best calibration tooling. Free, and better than most stock slicers.
  • Digital Calipers ⭐$
    You cannot design functional parts without measuring. Buy one decent pair, not three cheap ones.
  • MakerWorldFree
    Huge, mostly-free model library with one-click print profiles for Bambu machines. Start here before modeling anything.

🤖 AI & Local LLMs

Run models on your own hardware, and drive Claude & ChatGPT like a pro. The same 'own it, don't rent it' ethos as the rest of the list.

Run AI Locally
  • Ollama ⭐Free
    Run open LLMs locally with one command. The easiest on-ramp to your own private AI.
  • LM StudioFree
    A polished desktop app for downloading and chatting with local models. Great for non-terminal folks.
  • Open WebUIFree
    Self-hosted ChatGPT-style front end for Ollama and any OpenAI-compatible API. Runs beautifully on the homelab.
  • JanFree
    Open-source, offline-first AI assistant. Everything stays on your machine.
Hardware for Local AI
  • Mac mini (Apple Silicon) ⭐$$
    Unified memory makes it a shockingly good, quiet local-LLM box. The value pick for running mid-size models.
  • NVIDIA GeForce RTX (VRAM is king)$$$
    For local AI, buy the most VRAM you can afford. A used 3090's 24GB still punches above its price.
  • NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano$$
    A tiny always-on edge box for running small models and vision at the edge of your network.
Manage Claude & ChatGPT
  • Claude Code ⭐Free / Sub
    Drive Claude from your terminal to build, edit, and automate. The power-user way to work with Claude.
  • ChatGPTFree / Sub
    Custom instructions + Projects + memory turn it from a toy into a real assistant. Learn those three and it changes.
  • CursorFree / Sub
    AI-native code editor. If you write code, this is the fastest on-ramp to model-assisted work.
  • LibreChatFree
    Self-hosted, multi-model chat UI — bring your own API keys for Claude, GPT, and local models in one place.
Learn AI
  • AI Engineering (Chip Huyen) ⭐$$
    The field guide to building real products on top of foundation models. Start here if you're going pro.
  • Hands-On Large Language Models$$
    The best illustrated, practical intro to how LLMs actually work and how to use them.
  • DeepLearning.AI Short CoursesFree
    Free, focused, hour-long courses on prompting, RAG, agents, and more. The fastest way to skill up.
  • fast.aiFree
    The legendary free, top-down deep-learning course. Rigorous without being academic-for-its-own-sake.
