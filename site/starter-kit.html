The Self-Hosted Home Starter Kit

A no-fluff buying guide for starting a local smart home without filling your house with disposable cloud gadgets.

The core idea: buy local-first and buy things you own. Your essential systems should keep working when the internet—or the vendor—doesn't.

Start with these three

  1. A Home Assistant hub — the brain. Start plug-and-play; don't over-build on day one.
  2. A Zigbee/Thread radio — keeps sensors independent from any single vendor cloud.
  3. Contact and motion sensors — enough to automate one room and learn what you actually need.

Five expensive beginner mistakes

  1. Buying Wi-Fi everything. Prefer Zigbee, Thread, Matter, or wired PoE where practical.
  2. Skipping the UPS. A power blip can corrupt a NAS or storage card.
  3. Running from an SD card long-term. Move important services to SSD or NVMe.
  4. Ignoring wet filament. A dryer often fixes stringing and brittle prints.
  5. Keeping no off-box backup. Use a 3-2-1 backup plan for anything irreplaceable.

The ten-minute setup order

Bring the hub online → connect the radio through a USB extension → pair two or three sensors → build one automation you will use every day → only then add more devices.

One working automation beats twenty gadgets sitting unpaired.
