## How to add a guide (keep it passive)

1. Drop a new `.md` file in this folder.
2. Write original content. If you're drawing on your own coursework or notes,
   **synthesize** — don't paste copyrighted course material. The value is your
   take, not a re-upload of someone's lecture.
3. Add it to the list above and (optionally) feature it on the site's
   "From the workbench" section.
4. Link out to relevant picks so the article can earn.

> **A note on sourcing:** guides here may be informed by the author's AI
> coursework and hands-on projects. Course materials themselves are kept private
> and are never reproduced — only original synthesis is published.

## The SEO play

The articles that compound are **specific, evergreen, and answer a real query**:

- "How to give ChatGPT custom instructions that actually stick"
- "Claude Projects vs ChatGPT Projects: which for what"
- "Running your first local LLM on a Mac mini"

Each becomes a page that ranks for years and funnels readers to the list and the
email signup. One good evergreen guide beats ten hot-take posts.
